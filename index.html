Raising Seafarer Welfare With Iran's Port & Maritime Organization (PMO)

Tehran, Iran 10 July 2019
PMO Conference Hall
  • 01Directors Capt. Ujjawal B. Choudhary and management representatives met Iranian owners and officials from the Port and Maritime Organization (PMO) to discuss seafarer rights, safety, and welfare.
  • 02Discussions focused on the Collective Bargaining Agreement (CBA), maritime safety protocols, and recruitment exclusively through accredited agencies.
  • 03PMO agreed to an online grievance portal and strict enforcement of MLC 2006 on salary disbursement, sign-off schedules, and repatriation guarantees.
  • 04An initiative for comprehensive seafarer insurance and port-state welfare was accepted by Iranian shipowners.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What nationalities and vessel types do you recruit for? +
We recruit complete crews across all ranks: Master, Chief Officer, 2nd/3rd Officers, Chief Engineer, 2nd/3rd/4th Engineers, Electro-Technical Officers (ETO), Bosun, AB, OS, Fitter, Pumpman, and Chief Cook. Our experience spans Bulk Carriers, Product, Chemical & Crude Oil Tankers, Gas Carriers (LPG/Ethylene), Offshore DP Vessels (AHTS/PSV), Container Ships, and Tugs.
How does Sea & Seas verify sea-time and certificate authenticity? +
Every candidate undergoes a multi-tier verification process: biometric INDOS & CDC cross-verification via government databases, direct sea-service confirmation with previous vessel managers, and technical competency interviews conducted by our in-house Master Mariners and Chief Engineers.
What is the typical crew mobilization turnaround time? +
With our pre-vetted roster of officers and ratings, emergency crew mobilization can take place within 48 to 72 hours for Indian, Gulf, and Singapore ports. For planned rotations and full crew complements, standard turnaround is 7 to 14 working days including Flag State endorsements, visa processing, and medicals.
What port agency services do you provide in India and the UAE? +
We provide 24/7 port representation in Mumbai, JNPT, Kandla, Mundra, Pipavav, Cochin, Chennai, and Vizag (India), as well as Ajman, Sharjah, Sharjah Khalid Port, and Hamriyah (UAE). Services include customs clearance, berthing assistance, cargo-operation coordination, stevedoring support, crew sign-on/sign-off repatriation, bunker supply, freshwater, marine spares clearance, and ship husbandry.
Do you provide real-time software access for shipowners? +
Yes. Sea & Seas operates a proprietary web-based crew administration system that allows shipowners and technical managers 24/7 online access to crew profiles, matrix compliance, appraisal reports, travel details, and contract timelines.
